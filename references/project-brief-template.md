## Direction
- **Approach / style:**

- **Constraints:** deadline, tools, budget, compatibility, length, etc.

## Decisions
### Confirmed
- Decisions explicitly provided or approved by the user.

### Assumptions
- Reasonable choices made by the assistant that may matter to the result.

### Open questions
- Only unresolved questions that materially block progress.
- If none, write “None currently blocking.”

## Status
- **Current stage:** Discovery / Planning / Drafting / Production / Review / Complete
- **Next action:**
- **Change notes:** optional; record meaningful scope or direction changes.

## Completion checklist
- Deliverables match the agreed scope.
- Required constraints and content are satisfied.
- Important assumptions or limitations are disclosed.
- The user has a clear next step or usable handoff.
